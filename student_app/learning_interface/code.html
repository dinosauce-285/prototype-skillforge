04. The Golden Ratio in Digital Layouts

In this lesson, we explore how mathematical constants define the structures of the most celebrated designs in history. We'll move from classical architecture into contemporary UI design, understanding how to apply the 1.618 ratio to font scales, column widths, and spacing systems.

Key Takeaway

Visual harmony is calculated, not just felt.
