The Precision Architect Strategy

In this submission, I have focused on the "No-Line" rule for structural containment. By utilizing varying surface levels—specifically surface_container for the background and surface_container_lowest for the workspace—I've achieved visual separation without cluttering the interface with rigid border lines.

The typography dialogue between Manrope and Inter has been balanced to ensure legibility. I used Manrope for the "Wayfinder" headers at a display-md scale with tight tracking (-0.02em) to maintain the premium architect aesthetic.
