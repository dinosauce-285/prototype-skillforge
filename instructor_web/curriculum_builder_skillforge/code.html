Introduction to the Course

Welcome to the Foundations of UX Design. In this module, we'll explore why user experience has become the most critical differentiator in modern product development.

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Define User Experience (UX) and its history.
  • Identify the five stages of the Design Thinking process.
  • Explain the difference between UX and UI.
